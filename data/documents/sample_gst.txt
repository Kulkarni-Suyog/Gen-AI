
GSTSARAL - SAMPLE GST DOCUMENT

GST stands for Goods and Services Tax.

GST is an indirect tax applicable to the supply
of goods and services in India.

There are three major types of GST:

1. CGST - Central Goods and Services Tax.
2. SGST - State Goods and Services Tax.
3. IGST - Integrated Goods and Services Tax.

CGST and SGST generally apply to intra-state
supplies, while IGST generally applies to
inter-state supplies.

Input Tax Credit allows eligible registered
taxpayers to claim credit for GST paid on
eligible business purchases, subject to
applicable legal conditions.
